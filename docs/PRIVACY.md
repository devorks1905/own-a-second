# Privacy Policy — OWN A SECOND

**Last updated:** August 15, 2026

## 1. Overview
This Privacy Policy explains what information OWN A SECOND ("the Service") collects and how it is used.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Content you submit:** your chosen display name and message.
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser type, and approximate language, processed transiently for security, rate-limiting, and language detection.
- **Payment data:** the public wallet address you send from (visible on-chain) and the transaction identifier, used to confirm payment.
- We do **not** collect passwords, private keys, or seed phrases. We never ask for them.

## 3. How We Use Information
- To provide and operate the Service (displaying your message).
- To prevent abuse, fraud, and spam (rate-limiting, moderation).
- To comply with legal obligations (e.g., retaining records where required by law).

## 4. Legal Basis (GDPR)
Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, we process personal data on the basis of: (a) performance of a contract (providing the Service you requested); (b) legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention); and (c) consent where required.

## 5. Data Retention
- Messages remain displayed for as long as the associated Second is active, and may be retained for record-keeping.
- Technical logs (IP, moderation logs) are retained for security and legal purposes for a limited period.

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR / KVKK)
Where applicable, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact the operator.

## 7. Cookies
The Service uses minimal local storage (your language preference and interaction state) and does not use third-party advertising cookies.

## 8. Children
The Service is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 9. Third Parties
We may use service providers (hosting, database, translation) to operate the Service. They process data only as necessary to provide their services.

## 10. Contact
For privacy requests, contact the operator through the channels listed on the Service.
